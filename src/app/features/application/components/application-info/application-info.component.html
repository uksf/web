Information

Thanks for your interest in UKSF. This page tells you what being a member is like, so you know what you are signing up for. If anything is unclear, ask us on Discord.


When we play

Main ops: Saturday from 19:30 until late

Training: Wednesday from 19:30 until about 22:00

Side ops: optional, and sometimes on other evenings


How we play

British Special Forces operations, played with British Military procedures and a debrief after every op.

We have run since 2011, and many of our members have been here for 7 to 10 years. We look for members who want to stay.


What we ask of you

You must be 18 or older.

Make about 3 main ops a month. If you cannot make one, or a Wednesday training, post a LOA on the website before noon that day. Three absences in a row can lead to a discharge.

Arma 3 and the APEX DLC, with no other DLC needed, on a PC that runs the game well above vanilla performance.

TeamSpeak 3 and a working microphone.

One milsim unit at a time. Speak up, take the initiative, and use common sense.


After you join

Basic Training: 4 phases, at least 4 weeks.

Then SFSG, where you will train for troop skills.

After that, you can train for a role, such as NCO, Officer, or Team Medic, or for a specialist unit: Medic, RTO/FAC, Sniper, JSFAW, or Mortars.

Questions? Ask us on Discord or TeamSpeak (uk-sf.co.uk).
